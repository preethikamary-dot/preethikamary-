## Steps
1. Log in at gumroad.com and go to **Products → New product**.
2. Choose **Digital product**, enter the name and $2.99 price, and click **Next: Customize**.
3. Paste the description, upload the cover and thumbnail, and set the URL slug to `color-and-smile`.
4. On the **Content** tab, upload the PDF.
5. Click **Publish and continue**, then copy the product link
   (it looks like `https://<your-username>.gumroad.com/l/color-and-smile`).
6. Send the link to Claude, or paste it into `download: ""` near the top of `docs/index.html`.
