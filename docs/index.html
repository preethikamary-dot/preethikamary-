Questions

Good to know

What age is it for?

Ages 3 to 5. The big shapes also suit 2-year-olds coloring with a grown-up and early learners up to age 6.

Will markers bleed through?

Pages are printed on one side only, so bleed-through never spoils another picture. Slip a sheet of card behind the page if you use felt-tip markers.

How do I use the online coloring studio?

Pick a picture, tap a color, then tap any shape. Use Undo if you change your mind and Start over to begin again. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

How were the pictures made?

The line art was created with the help of AI and then refined by hand for thick, closed lines and big spaces that suit young children.

Can I print pages from the Kindle edition?

Yes. Open the eBook on a computer or tablet and print the pages you like for personal use at home.
